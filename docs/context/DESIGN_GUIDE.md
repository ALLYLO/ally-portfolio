# Design guide

No historical chat proposal has yet been confirmed as a durable project-wide design constraint for this guide.

For the current visual and interaction implementation, inspect index.html and the relevant files in assets/ and prototypes/. Existing implementation is evidence of what is present, not proof that every choice is permanently approved. Keep unapproved alternatives in OPEN_QUESTIONS.md or outside the public repository; add a durable rule here only after the Portfolio owner explicitly confirms it.

In simplified Chinese, display “项目经历” (WO → 项, R → 目, KS → 经历) with all four characters solid ivory (#f5f0e8), without the English-only outline effect, and with one consistent headline weight. ## Page scroll behavior

Hide the root browser scrollbar visually without disabling vertical page scrolling. Use restrained desktop wheel easing and a modest reduction in scroll distance; preserve native mobile touch scrolling, keyboard navigation, nested case-study/dialog scrolling, and reduced-motion preferences. Avoid replacing the page with a transformed scrolling container or locking normal navigation.

## Pointer trail

The pointer trail uses a single underdamped spring and one continuous Bézier path, never layered strands. The follower keeps advancing to its latest mouse position until settled, independently of the 220 ms ink fade. Leaving the viewport and returning preserves the last spring position, so re-entry traces from that position instead of teleporting; neither idle time nor a large pointer jump resets it. A fresh page load initializes at the first mouse event without a streak. Render the enhanced canvas as white ink with mix-blend-mode: difference to invert pixels beneath the trail across light and dark areas. Use smooth per-segment alpha gradients to give the leading edge higher contrast and the trailing edge a continuous fade, preserving the 220 ms ink lifetime. Keep line-width taper, cursor appearance, and reduced-motion/mobile safeguards; the legacy inline trail remains the fallback only until enhancement initialization. Do not alter unrelated interactions.

## Narrow-screen homepage and navigation

At widths below 600px, where the full editorial two-column hero has not yet been enabled, the homepage displays its title first, then the portrait collage, followed by the thesis (“Understand What Matters / Clarify What Comes Next”), Work and Resume actions, and existing supporting cards. The narrow-screen header keeps the Ally Lo wordmark and replaces the visible Home, Work, Skills and Let's Connect links with an accessible right-side hamburger. Its full-screen editorial overlay reuses the desktop section URLs and existing contact destinations. On wider screens preserve the previous header and hero layout. Keep keyboard close and focus behavior, reduced-motion support and natural mobile scrolling.

The mobile header and menu icon are rendered as white source ink under difference blending, so scrolling dark typography beneath the wordmark produces an actual inverse rather than flat dark-on-dark overlap. Do not eliminate the intended scroll overlap. Narrow-screen headline typography remains slightly restrained, with a modest gap below the wordmark at rest while retaining the intentional Logo/title overlap during scrolling. Keep the source portrait collage unchanged, remove the redundant mobile CSS paper backing, and crop the peripheral collage decorations around the subject with mobile-only framing; display the portrait about one size smaller and slightly reduce the narrow-screen thesis typography for a calmer hierarchy. At every viewport width, style VIEW WORK as a thin-outlined pill and VIEW RESUME as a lightweight underlined text link with its existing arrow, never a second pill. Preserve existing labels, destinations and disabled state; keep layout, background texture and imagery unchanged.

## Responsive layout quality

Use content-safe grid tracks and fluid proportional placement for the editorial homepage instead of hard-coded portrait positions. Preserve the wide-screen visual direction but maintain coherent content grouping while a desktop viewport shrinks. The mobile, tablet and desktop transitions must keep the heading, portrait, thesis, CTAs and supporting cards within their available width, without clipping or unwanted horizontal scrolling. At widths below 600px, keep the portrait-first stacked composition. At 600px and above, keep the portrait physically nested in its collage but create a grouping around the original thesis and buttons. At 600–1399px, flatten the wrappers for a full-width title followed by a two-column thesis/actions and portrait row, with a three-card row beneath. At 600–899px, scale the type, image, CTA spacing and supporting cards without collapsing the grid into a single column. At 1400px and above restore the original wide desktop left-copy / right-collage grid. Preserve every CTA and its original destination. At 600px and above, the editorial header displays its original Home, Work, Skills and Let's Connect destinations; only narrower viewports use the mobile overlay. From 600px onward the three paper cards always appear side by side. At 600–1399px the expanded stage follows a full-width editorial headline, a second row with grouped thesis/actions on the left and a contained portrait on the right, and three cards beneath; 1400px and above restores the full desktop left-copy/right-collage composition. Preserve the original wide-screen editorial style, colors and interactions. Test at representative widths and by continuously resizing, not just two screenshots.

Do not allow the desktop portrait to be positioned relative to the whole hero and spill across the left column. Physically nest it in the right collage and reserve the card row below it. The tablet/desktop view may have natural text wrapping in its narrower left column. Treat the 599/600px content and navigation transition as a visual QA boundary and keep the portrait crop and text scale visually coherent at both.

When the viewport is too short to show both the portrait and actions in a stacked hero, reduce the portrait's vertical space without disabling natural scrolling. The desktop composition should engage early enough to avoid a very large mid-width portrait with the primary actions stranded at the fold. Do not imply that every action can be visible on every physically tiny screen.

At 600–959px the site uses a compact three-column header for wordmark, Home/Work/Skills links, and Let's Connect. This full navigation appears from the same 600px threshold as the split composition; CSS and navigation script use the identical 599/600px boundary. At 600–1399px display three resized paper cards in a full-width row beneath the main content; 1400px and above restores their location inside the right collage beneath the portrait. Always reserve at least one small-viewport height for the homepage hero so the WORKS section does not appear until the user scrolls, regardless of window width. Do not hide WORKS with JavaScript or block regular scrolling. The portrait must never overlap the left column or the CTA hit targets.

The two-column expanded (600–1399px) stage is intentionally distinct from both narrow mobile and fully wide desktop: the headline spans the page first (as in the user's reference), before the introduction and portrait split into two columns. CSS-only scale changes cannot achieve this when the title and CTAs remain a single grid item; the grouping must exist in DOM without duplicating headings, links, or cards. The three paper cards remain one row under that composition. Homepage should reserve at least a viewport height before WORKS, including for short windows. The navigation and hero both expand at 600px. Within the 600–1399px split hero, size the description and both actions fluidly, allowing the CTAs to wrap when necessary, without a sudden decrease at 900px or 960px.

The two-column expanded hero (600–1399px) uses one shared, height-aware spacing budget for the full-width headline, the paired intro/portrait row, and the smaller supporting-card band. At 600–1199px on short browser windows, reduce headline, portrait and gaps without collapsing the columns; allow normal scrolling if the height genuinely cannot accommodate all content. Prefer fluid horizontal and vertical constraints (vw and svh) rather than unrelated margins at each breakpoint. Keep the collage portrait within its right track and visibly subordinate to the headline. Three cards stay in one row but are reduced in width, height, and internal illustration scale. Keep the outlined Work CTA and underlined Resume link internally aligned, and allow wrapping only when there is genuinely not enough room for both. Legacy pre-structured desktop styles must not continue overriding the current structured layout: fallback rules target only layouts without the structured class. This is a visual rhythm refinement; preserve copy, destinations, and the original photo asset.

## Fully expanded first-viewport fit

In the full-width desktop hero (1400px and above), the fixed header already reserves 135px of page height. Size the homepage to the **remaining viewport** rather than an additional 100svh. Both columns must fit the title, thesis, both CTAs, portrait, and all three paper cards without clipping or requiring a scroll at conventional desktop viewport sizes (1440×800, 1600×900, 1920×1080). Align left-hand information as a coherent group and use two explicit rows in the right collage: a flexible portrait row and a full three-card row. Set picture size, heading, and card height using both viewport width and height constraints. The WORKS section follows after the initial hero, never before it. On physically very short desktop windows where this is impossible without unreadable text, allow ordinary scrolling instead of cutting elements off or hiding cards. Retain the original art and links.

## Narrow-screen overlay menu animation

The portrait-width menu overlay remains an accessible full-screen navigation on viewports below 600px. Its opening visual sequence uses a warm parchment background slightly darker than the main ivory crumpled-paper hero, followed by three masked upward-reveal headline links (Home / Work / Skills) with staggered delays, and then a quieter footer reveal. Make the central list editorial and compact rather than oversized, with small superscript numbers, generous surrounding space, a round close affordance, optional real social destinations shown as small outlined circles, and the existing contact/email destinations at the bottom. Use a restrained champagne/ink palette with subtle blue hover accent; never fabricate social links or change destination URLs. Closing preserves Escape, focus management, inert state, scroll locking, and reduced-motion support. Keep this styling restricted to the mobile overlay; do not affect the wide desktop header or hero composition.

## Bilingual portfolio presentation

The Portfolio offers a reversible English / 简体中文 interface language switch. Keep English as the canonical authored source; translate presentation copy in place instead of duplicating project pages. Place a discreet EN / 中文 control near the desktop contact navigation, and a matching control inside the mobile navigation overlay. Retain the visitor's preference locally and update document language, accessible control labels, and page metadata. All existing links, metrics, brand names, source images, screenshots, original Japanese outreach samples, downloadable assets, and any required evidence/disclosure qualifiers remain unchanged. Translate the static homepage, work list, skills and credentials, contact area, and text inserted by the main case-study renderer. Fall back to the original language for text that has not been reviewed; do not present machine-translated or fabricated business claims as approved. Check both translation directions, language persistence, mobile-menu timing, dynamic dialogs, accessible focus, and major responsive widths.

In the fully expanded desktop hero, avoid making the page look sparse merely to keep everything above the fold. Use a slightly top-biased left headline/thesis/CTA group and a portrait aligned toward that group while retaining the independent right collage grid. Keep the paper cards comfortably above the viewport bottom and legible as the third level in the hierarchy, with extra room for the circular skill labels in both languages. For Chinese, adjust the headline's actual ideographic glyph scale and the card Venn typography independently; do not force identical Latin and Han glyph sizing. The first-viewport fit and WORKS-below-fold requirements remain in force at 1440×800, 1600×900, and 1920×1080, with natural scrolling on abnormally short windows. These proportion and spacing adjustments should remain scoped to the fully expanded (>=1400px) mode rather than changing intermediate or mobile layouts.
