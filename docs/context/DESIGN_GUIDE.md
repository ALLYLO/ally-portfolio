For the current visual and interaction implementation, inspect index.html and the relevant files in assets/ and prototypes/. Existing implementation is evidence of what is present, not proof that every choice is permanently approved. Keep unapproved alternatives in OPEN_QUESTIONS.md or outside the public repository; add a durable rule here only after the Portfolio owner explicitly confirms it.

The detail area should show the HoYoverse and Reverse: 1999 summaries together without its own scrollbar. On narrow screens, preserve complete text in the normal page flow rather than clipping content to force a fixed-height panel.

The Japan Creator Marketing card on the Works page presents both programs' headline outcomes in one compact bottom bar without enlarging it. When Works cards appear side by side, their visual frames should have equal heights so the project titles align. Fit the Japan Creator Marketing image area around its outcome bar within that shared frame height.

## NIKKE detail view

Use the existing case modal, roughly two-thirds content and one-third project introduction on desktop, with the shared top and Previous/Next navigation. Four internal pages separate the Operating System, Operations Evidence, Creator Activation, and Community & Follow-through so each page fits a normal desktop viewport without an inner vertical scrollbar. ## Page scroll behavior

Hide the root browser scrollbar visually without disabling vertical page scrolling. Use restrained desktop wheel easing and a modest reduction in scroll distance; preserve native mobile touch scrolling, keyboard navigation, nested case-study/dialog scrolling, and reduced-motion preferences. Avoid replacing the page with a transformed scrolling container or locking normal navigation.

## Pointer trail

The pointer trail uses a single underdamped spring and one continuous Bézier path, never layered strands. The follower keeps advancing to its latest mouse position until settled, independently of the 220 ms ink fade. Leaving the viewport and returning preserves the last spring position, so re-entry traces from that position instead of teleporting; neither idle time nor a large pointer jump resets it. A fresh page load initializes at the first mouse event without a streak. Render the enhanced canvas as white ink with mix-blend-mode: difference to invert pixels beneath the trail across light and dark areas. Use smooth per-segment alpha gradients to give the leading edge higher contrast and the trailing edge a continuous fade, preserving the 220 ms ink lifetime. Keep line-width taper, cursor appearance, and reduced-motion/mobile safeguards; the legacy inline trail remains the fallback only until enhancement initialization. Do not alter unrelated interactions.

## Narrow-screen homepage and navigation

At widths up to 739px, where the full editorial two-column hero has not yet been enabled, the homepage displays its title first, then the portrait collage, followed by the thesis (“Understand What Matters / Clarify What Comes Next”), Work and Resume actions, and existing supporting cards. The narrow-screen header keeps the Ally Lo wordmark and replaces the visible Home, Work, Skills and Let's Connect links with an accessible right-side hamburger. Its full-screen editorial overlay reuses the desktop section URLs and existing contact destinations. On wider screens preserve the previous header and hero layout. Keep keyboard close and focus behavior, reduced-motion support and natural mobile scrolling.

The mobile header and menu icon are rendered as white source ink under difference blending, so scrolling dark typography beneath the wordmark produces an actual inverse rather than flat dark-on-dark overlap. Do not eliminate the intended scroll overlap. Narrow-screen headline typography remains slightly restrained, with a modest gap below the wordmark at rest while retaining the intentional Logo/title overlap during scrolling. Keep the source portrait collage unchanged, remove the redundant mobile CSS paper backing, and crop the peripheral collage decorations around the subject with mobile-only framing; display the portrait about one size smaller and slightly reduce the narrow-screen thesis typography for a calmer hierarchy. In narrow screens only, style VIEW WORK as a single thin-outlined pill and VIEW RESUME as a lightweight underlined text link, preserving the existing labels, destinations, and disabled state. Desktop layout, background texture, and copy stay as before.

## Responsive layout quality

Use content-safe grid tracks and fluid proportional placement for the editorial homepage instead of hard-coded portrait positions. Preserve the wide-screen visual direction but maintain coherent content grouping while a desktop viewport shrinks. The mobile, tablet and desktop transitions must keep the heading, portrait, thesis, CTAs and supporting cards within their available width, without clipping or unwanted horizontal scrolling. At widths below 600px, keep the portrait-first stacked composition. At 600px and above, keep the portrait physically nested in its collage but create a grouping around the original thesis and buttons. At 600–1399px, flatten those wrappers for a full-width title row followed by a two-column thesis+buttons / portrait row and a full-width three-card row. At 1400px and above restore the original wide desktop left-copy / right-collage grid. Preserve every CTA and its original destination. The menu stays collapsed until 959px because navigation needs more width than the two-column content. From 600px onward the three paper cards always appear side by side. At 600–1399px the first expanded stage follows a full-width editorial headline, then a second row with the grouped thesis/actions on the left and a contained portrait on the right, followed by three scaled cards in one full-width horizontal row; only at 1400px and above does the full desktop left-copy/right-collage composition return. Preserve the original wide-screen editorial style, colors and interactions. Test at representative widths and by continuously resizing, not just two screenshots.

Do not allow the desktop portrait to be positioned relative to the whole hero and spill across the left column. Physically nest it in the right collage and reserve the card row below it. The tablet/desktop view may have natural text wrapping in its narrower left column. Treat the 599/600px and 959/960px transitions as visual QA boundaries and keep the portrait crop and text scale visually coherent at both.

When the viewport is too short to show both the portrait and actions in a stacked hero, reduce the portrait's vertical space without disabling natural scrolling. The desktop composition should engage early enough to avoid a very large mid-width portrait with the primary actions stranded at the fold. Do not imply that every action can be visible on every physically tiny screen.

At 600–959px the page adopts compact two-column content while retaining the collapsed mobile navigation. Match the header's CSS breakpoint to the navigation script independently from the hero content breakpoint. At 600–1399px display three resized paper cards in one full-width row following the text/photo row; 1400px and above restores their location inside the right collage beneath the portrait. Always reserve at least one small-viewport height for the homepage hero so the WORKS section does not appear until the user scrolls, regardless of window width. Do not hide WORKS with JavaScript or block regular scrolling. The portrait must never overlap the left column or the CTA hit targets.

The initially expanded (600–1399px) stage is intentionally distinct from both narrow mobile and fully wide desktop: the headline spans the page first (as in the user's reference), before the introduction and portrait split into two columns. CSS-only scale changes cannot achieve this when the title and CTAs remain a single grid item; the grouping must exist in DOM without duplicating headings, links, or cards. The three paper cards remain one row under that composition. Homepage should reserve at least a viewport height before WORKS, including for short windows. The navigation breakpoint remains independent at 960px.
