For the current visual and interaction implementation, inspect index.html and the relevant files in assets/ and prototypes/. Existing implementation is evidence of what is present, not proof that every choice is permanently approved. Keep unapproved alternatives in OPEN_QUESTIONS.md or outside the public repository; add a durable rule here only after the Portfolio owner explicitly confirms it.

## Page scroll behavior

Hide the root browser scrollbar visually without disabling vertical page scrolling. Use restrained desktop wheel easing and a modest reduction in scroll distance; preserve native mobile touch scrolling, keyboard navigation, nested case-study/dialog scrolling, and reduced-motion preferences. Avoid replacing the page with a transformed scrolling container or locking normal navigation.

## Pointer trail

The pointer trail uses a single underdamped spring and one continuous Bézier path, never layered strands. The follower keeps advancing to its latest mouse position until settled, independently of the 220 ms ink fade. Leaving the viewport and returning preserves the last spring position, so re-entry traces from that position instead of teleporting; neither idle time nor a large pointer jump resets it. A fresh page load initializes at the first mouse event without a streak. Render the enhanced canvas as white ink with mix-blend-mode: difference to invert pixels beneath the trail across light and dark areas. Use smooth per-segment alpha gradients to give the leading edge higher contrast and the trailing edge a continuous fade, preserving the 220 ms ink lifetime. Keep line-width taper, cursor appearance, and reduced-motion/mobile safeguards; the legacy inline trail remains the fallback only until enhancement initialization. Do not alter unrelated interactions.

## Narrow-screen homepage and navigation

At widths below 600px, where the full editorial two-column hero has not yet been enabled, the homepage displays its title first, then the portrait collage, followed by the thesis (“Understand What Matters / Clarify What Comes Next”), Work and Resume actions, and existing supporting cards. The narrow-screen header keeps the Ally Lo wordmark and replaces the visible Home, Work, Skills and Let's Connect links with an accessible right-side hamburger. Its full-screen editorial overlay reuses the desktop section URLs and existing contact destinations. On wider screens preserve the previous header and hero layout. Keep keyboard close and focus behavior, reduced-motion support and natural mobile scrolling.

The mobile header and menu icon are rendered as white source ink under difference blending, so scrolling dark typography beneath the wordmark produces an actual inverse rather than flat dark-on-dark overlap. Do not eliminate the intended scroll overlap. Narrow-screen headline typography remains slightly restrained, with a modest gap below the wordmark at rest while retaining the intentional Logo/title overlap during scrolling. Keep the source portrait collage unchanged, remove the redundant mobile CSS paper backing, and crop the peripheral collage decorations around the subject with mobile-only framing; display the portrait about one size smaller and slightly reduce the narrow-screen thesis typography for a calmer hierarchy. In narrow screens only, style VIEW WORK as a single thin-outlined pill and VIEW RESUME as a lightweight underlined text link, preserving the existing labels, destinations, and disabled state. Desktop layout, background texture, and copy stay as before.

## Responsive layout quality

Use content-safe grid tracks and fluid proportional placement for the editorial homepage instead of hard-coded portrait positions. Preserve the wide-screen visual direction but maintain coherent content grouping while a desktop viewport shrinks. The mobile, tablet and desktop transitions must keep the heading, portrait, thesis, CTAs and supporting cards within their available width, without clipping or unwanted horizontal scrolling. At widths below 600px, keep the portrait-first stacked composition. At 600px and above, keep the portrait physically nested in its collage but create a grouping around the original thesis and buttons. At 600–1399px, flatten those wrappers for a full-width title row followed by a two-column thesis+buttons / portrait row and a full-width three-card row. At 1400px and above restore the original wide desktop left-copy / right-collage grid. Preserve every CTA and its original destination. At 600px and above, the editorial header displays its original Home, Work, Skills and Let's Connect destinations; only narrower viewports use the mobile overlay. From 600px onward the three paper cards always appear side by side. At 600–1399px the first expanded stage follows a full-width editorial headline, then a second row with the grouped thesis/actions on the left and a contained portrait on the right, followed by three scaled cards in one full-width horizontal row; only at 1400px and above does the full desktop left-copy/right-collage composition return. Preserve the original wide-screen editorial style, colors and interactions. Test at representative widths and by continuously resizing, not just two screenshots.

Do not allow the desktop portrait to be positioned relative to the whole hero and spill across the left column. Physically nest it in the right collage and reserve the card row below it. The tablet/desktop view may have natural text wrapping in its narrower left column. Treat the 599/600px content and navigation transition as a visual QA boundary and keep the portrait crop and text scale visually coherent at both.

When the viewport is too short to show both the portrait and actions in a stacked hero, reduce the portrait's vertical space without disabling natural scrolling. The desktop composition should engage early enough to avoid a very large mid-width portrait with the primary actions stranded at the fold. Do not imply that every action can be visible on every physically tiny screen.

At 600–959px the first expanded hero uses a compact three-column header for wordmark, Home/Work/Skills links, and Let's Connect. This full navigation appears from the same 600px threshold as the split composition; CSS and navigation script use the identical 599/600px boundary. At 600–1399px display three resized paper cards in one full-width row following the text/photo row; 1400px and above restores their location inside the right collage beneath the portrait. Always reserve at least one small-viewport height for the homepage hero so the WORKS section does not appear until the user scrolls, regardless of window width. Do not hide WORKS with JavaScript or block regular scrolling. The portrait must never overlap the left column or the CTA hit targets.

The initially expanded (600–1399px) stage is intentionally distinct from both narrow mobile and fully wide desktop: the headline spans the page first (as in the user's reference), before the introduction and portrait split into two columns. CSS-only scale changes cannot achieve this when the title and CTAs remain a single grid item; the grouping must exist in DOM without duplicating headings, links, or cards. The three paper cards remain one row under that composition. Homepage should reserve at least a viewport height before WORKS, including for short windows. The navigation and first expanded hero now share the 599/600px boundary. When the hero splits into two columns, its description and both primary actions are enlarged using continuous fluid sizing throughout the full 600–1399px expanded stage, so neither abruptly shrinks at 960px.

The first expanded hero (600–1399px) uses one shared, height-aware spacing budget for the full-width headline, the paired intro/portrait row, and the smaller supporting-card band. Prefer fluid horizontal and vertical constraints (vw and svh) rather than unrelated margins at each breakpoint. Keep the collage portrait within its right track and visibly subordinate to the headline. Three cards stay in one row but are reduced in width, height, and internal illustration scale. Keep the two CTA pills internally aligned, and allow wrapping only when there is genuinely not enough room for both. Legacy pre-structured desktop styles must not continue overriding the current structured layout: fallback rules target only layouts without the structured class. This is a visual rhythm refinement; preserve copy, destinations, and the original photo asset.

## Fully expanded first-viewport fit

In the full-width desktop hero (1400px and above), the fixed header already reserves 135px of page height. Size the homepage to the **remaining viewport** rather than an additional 100svh. Both columns must fit the title, thesis, both CTAs, portrait, and all three paper cards without clipping or requiring a scroll at conventional desktop viewport sizes (1440×800, 1600×900, 1920×1080). Align left-hand information as a coherent group and use two explicit rows in the right collage: a flexible portrait row and a full three-card row. Set picture size, heading, and card height using both viewport width and height constraints. The WORKS section follows after the initial hero, never before it. On physically very short desktop windows where this is impossible without unreadable text, allow ordinary scrolling instead of cutting elements off or hiding cards. Retain the original art and links.
