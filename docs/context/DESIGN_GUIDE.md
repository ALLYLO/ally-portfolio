# Design guide

No historical chat proposal has yet been confirmed as a durable project-wide design constraint for this guide.

For the current visual and interaction implementation, inspect index.html and the relevant files in assets/ and prototypes/. Existing implementation is evidence of what is present, not proof that every choice is permanently approved. Keep unapproved alternatives in OPEN_QUESTIONS.md or outside the public repository; add a durable rule here only after the Portfolio owner explicitly confirms it.

## Multiscript serif typography

Every visible language view uses one script-aware serif system: Chinese Han characters use the site's self-hosted, OFL-licensed Noto Serif SC subset for consistent Song-style (宋体风格) glyphs across devices; English and Latin characters and all digits use the device's Times New Roman, falling back to Georgia; Japanese kana and kanji use Yu Mincho (游明朝). Unicode-range local font faces route each script independently; include Apple's installed `Times New Roman`, `YuMincho Medium` / `YuMincho Demibold`, and `YuMincho +36p Kana` local names so iOS Safari resolves the intended faces. The Noto Serif SC subset and its SIL Open Font License are kept in assets/fonts/; update the subset when Chinese site copy changes. Japanese passages with Han glyphs must also carry lang="ja"; Japanese outreach subject/body and creator names/titles are marked accordingly. Keep serif fallbacks (STSong/SimSun/Noto Serif SC for Chinese; Hiragino Mincho/Noto Serif JP for Japanese) for platforms if the bundled font cannot load. Do not bundle proprietary fonts or load the superseded Chinese Noto Sans webfonts. Preserve content, imagery, evidence, metrics, interactions, responsive breakpoints and the Chinese WORKS heading's ivory fill. Serif glyphs have different widths, so check text fit and spacing across viewports before approving visual quality.

## Page scroll behavior

Hide the root browser scrollbar visually without disabling vertical page scrolling. Use restrained desktop wheel easing and a modest reduction in scroll distance; preserve native mobile touch scrolling, keyboard navigation, nested case-study/dialog scrolling, and reduced-motion preferences. Avoid replacing the page with a transformed scrolling container or locking normal navigation.

## Pointer trail

The pointer trail uses a single underdamped spring and one continuous Bézier path, never layered strands. The follower keeps advancing to its latest mouse position until settled, independently of the 220 ms ink fade. Leaving the viewport and returning preserves the last spring position, so re-entry traces from that position instead of teleporting; neither idle time nor a large pointer jump resets it. A fresh page load initializes at the first mouse event without a streak. Render the enhanced canvas as white ink with mix-blend-mode: difference to invert pixels beneath the trail across light and dark areas. Use smooth per-segment alpha gradients to give the leading edge higher contrast and the trailing edge a continuous fade, preserving the 220 ms ink lifetime. Keep line-width taper, cursor appearance, and reduced-motion/mobile safeguards; the legacy inline trail remains the fallback only until enhancement initialization. Do not alter unrelated interactions.

## Four compact capability paper tags

The owner-approved hero replaces the three large Skills / Markets / Tools paper cards with four small paper tags around the original portrait: Insight / 信息洞察, Structured Thinking / 结构化思维, Alignment / 跨境协同, Execution / 项目推进. These describe transferable capabilities rather than job roles or new business outcomes. Use visibly tinted cream, ice-blue and champagne paper, short translated labels and simple line icons; do not add long descriptions. The paper texture SVG has an opaque ivory fill, so multiply it with each sticker tint rather than letting that fill wash the stickers out. Add restrained paper-tape accents, soft shadows and a secondary orbit ring behind the portrait without making the stickers resemble plain white buttons. Anchor the tags to the original portrait element, not the viewport, and keep a decorative orbit behind the image. Below 600px retain the title → portrait → thesis → CTA flow as a compact 100svh mobile first-screen flex composition, including safe-area padding and a height-limited portrait that absorbs available space; keep the fixed navigation distinct from the hero so the body header spacer does not consume an additional viewport height. Ensure subtitle and both CTAs are reachable without scrolling on typical phones while retaining natural scrolling under extreme zoom or unusually short heights; from 600–1399px retain the two-column hero but give its two-row grid enough height for the intro and portrait to occupy the available vertical space; at 1400px+ remove the redundant full card row. In the Chinese 600–1399px view, put the serif second thesis sentence on a separate line rather than breaking it awkwardly across the narrow left column. Preserve the original image and CTA links, keep all labels off the face and away from the left-side copy, avoid overflow and preserve bilingual and reduced-motion behavior. Keep original three-card markup only as a fallback if the enhancement cannot run.

## Responsive layout quality

Use content-safe grid tracks and fluid proportional placement for the editorial homepage instead of hard-coded portrait positions. Preserve the wide-screen visual direction but maintain coherent content grouping while a desktop viewport shrinks. The mobile, tablet and desktop transitions must keep the heading, portrait, thesis, CTAs and capability tags within their available width, without clipping or unwanted horizontal scrolling. At widths below 600px, keep the portrait-first stacked composition. At 600px and above, keep the portrait physically nested in its collage but create a grouping around the original thesis and buttons. At 600–1399px, flatten the wrappers for a full-width title followed by a two-column thesis/actions and portrait row, with capability paper tags attached to the portrait instead of a separate card row. At 600–899px, scale the type, image, CTA spacing and capability paper tags without collapsing the grid into a single column. At 1400px and above restore the original wide desktop left-copy / right-collage grid. Preserve every CTA and its original destination. At 600px and above, the editorial header displays its original Home, Work, Skills and Let's Connect destinations; only narrower viewports use the mobile overlay. From 600px onward the four capability tags orbit the portrait instead of occupying a separate card row. At 600–1399px the expanded stage follows a full-width editorial headline and a spacious second row with grouped thesis/actions on the left and a contained portrait on the right; 1400px and above restores the full desktop left-copy/right-collage composition. Preserve the original wide-screen editorial style, colors and interactions. Test at representative widths and by continuously resizing, not just two screenshots.

Do not allow the desktop portrait to be positioned relative to the whole hero and spill across the left column. Physically nest it in the right collage, with small capability tags around the portrait instead of a separate card row. The tablet/desktop view may have natural text wrapping in its narrower left column. Treat the 599/600px content and navigation transition as a visual QA boundary and keep the portrait crop and text scale visually coherent at both.

When the viewport is too short to show both the portrait and actions in a stacked hero, reduce the portrait's vertical space without disabling natural scrolling. The desktop composition should engage early enough to avoid a very large mid-width portrait with the primary actions stranded at the fold. Do not imply that every action can be visible on every physically tiny screen.

At 600–959px the site uses a compact three-column header for wordmark, Home/Work/Skills links, and Let's Connect. This full navigation appears from the same 600px threshold as the split composition; CSS and navigation script use the identical 599/600px boundary. At 600–1399px display four small capability tags anchored to the portrait in the right-hand column; 1400px and above allows more room for their orbit. Always reserve at least one small-viewport height for the homepage hero so the WORKS section does not appear until the user scrolls, regardless of window width. Do not hide WORKS with JavaScript or block regular scrolling. The portrait must never overlap the left column or the CTA hit targets.

The two-column expanded (600–1399px) stage is intentionally distinct from both narrow mobile and fully wide desktop: the headline spans the page first (as in the user's reference), before the introduction and portrait split into two columns. CSS-only scale changes cannot achieve this when the title and CTAs remain a single grid item; the grouping must exist in DOM without duplicating headings, links, or cards. The four paper tags remain attached to the portrait rather than occupying a separate row. Homepage should reserve at least a viewport height before WORKS, including for short windows. The navigation and hero both expand at 600px. Within the 600–1399px split hero, size the description and both actions fluidly, allowing the CTAs to wrap when necessary, without a sudden decrease at 900px or 960px.

The two-column expanded hero (600–1399px) uses one shared, height-aware spacing budget for the full-width headline, the paired intro/portrait row, and the compact capability orbit around the portrait. At 600–1199px on short browser windows, reduce headline, portrait and gaps without collapsing the columns; allow normal scrolling if the height genuinely cannot accommodate all content. Prefer fluid horizontal and vertical constraints (vw and svh) rather than unrelated margins at each breakpoint. Keep the collage portrait within its right track and visibly subordinate to the headline. Four small tags stay arranged around the portrait, while English labels can wrap rather than shrink to unreadable sizes. Keep the outlined Work CTA and underlined Resume link internally aligned, and allow wrapping only when there is genuinely not enough room for both. Legacy pre-structured desktop styles must not continue overriding the current structured layout: fallback rules target only layouts without the structured class. This is a visual rhythm refinement; preserve copy, destinations, and the original photo asset.

## Fully expanded first-viewport fit

In the full-width desktop hero (1400px and above), the fixed header already reserves 135px of page height. Size the homepage to the **remaining viewport** rather than an additional 100svh. Both columns must fit the title, thesis, both CTAs, portrait, and all four capability tags without clipping or requiring a scroll at conventional desktop viewport sizes (1440×800, 1600×900, 1920×1080). Align left-hand information as a coherent group and use a single portrait row with tags anchored around the image. Set picture size, heading and tag placement using viewport width and height constraints. The WORKS section follows after the initial hero, never before it. On physically very short desktop windows where this is impossible without unreadable text, allow ordinary scrolling instead of cutting elements off or hiding capability tags. Retain the original art and links.

## Narrow-screen overlay menu animation

The portrait-width menu overlay remains an accessible full-screen navigation on viewports below 600px. Its opening visual sequence uses a warm parchment background slightly darker than the main ivory crumpled-paper hero, followed by three masked upward-reveal headline links (Home / Work / Skills) with staggered delays, and then a quieter footer reveal. Make the central list editorial and compact rather than oversized, with small superscript numbers, generous surrounding space, a round close affordance, optional real social destinations shown as small outlined circles, and the existing contact/email destinations at the bottom. Use a restrained champagne/ink palette with subtle blue hover accent; never fabricate social links or change destination URLs. Closing preserves Escape, focus management, inert state, scroll locking, and reduced-motion support. Keep this styling restricted to the mobile overlay; do not affect the wide desktop header or hero composition.

## Bilingual portfolio presentation

The Portfolio offers a reversible English / 简体中文 interface language switch. Keep English as the canonical authored source; translate presentation copy in place instead of duplicating project pages. Place a discreet EN / 中文 control near the desktop contact navigation, and a matching control inside the mobile navigation overlay. Retain the visitor's preference locally and update document language, accessible control labels, and page metadata. All existing links, metrics, brand names, source images, screenshots, original Japanese outreach samples, downloadable assets, and any required evidence/disclosure qualifiers remain unchanged. Translate the static homepage, work list, skills and credentials, contact area, text inserted by the main case-study renderer, and the narrow-screen project summary and Browse Project action. Fall back to the original language for text that has not been reviewed; do not present machine-translated or fabricated business claims as approved. Check both translation directions, language persistence, mobile-menu timing, dynamic dialogs, accessible focus, and major responsive widths.

In the fully expanded desktop hero, avoid making the page look sparse merely to keep everything above the fold. Use a slightly top-biased left headline/thesis/CTA group and a portrait aligned toward that group while retaining the independent right collage grid. Keep the four compact capability tags legible around the portrait without obscuring the face or the core message. For Chinese, adjust the headline's actual ideographic glyph scale and the capability tag typography independently; do not force identical Latin and Han glyph sizing. The first-viewport fit and WORKS-below-fold requirements remain in force at 1440×800, 1600×900, and 1920×1080, with natural scrolling on abnormally short windows. These proportion and spacing adjustments should remain scoped to the fully expanded (>=1400px) mode rather than changing intermediate or mobile layouts.
